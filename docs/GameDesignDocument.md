# Vision

The Great War: THE GAME is a turn-based grand strategy game designed for eighth-grade students.

Players command one of the six major European powers during World War I and must manage diplomacy, military operations, industry, research, and public opinion while experiencing the historical challenges of the First World War.

The game should be historically accurate while remaining easy to learn and enjoyable to play on a Chromebook.

# Core Gameplay

Turn-based

One turn equals one month.

Players manage:

• Economy

• Production

• Diplomacy

• Military

• Research

• Historical Events

---

# Game Goal

Lead your nation to victory while understanding the historical causes and consequences of World War I.
